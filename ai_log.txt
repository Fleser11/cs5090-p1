- 2026-09-30: No code changes. Traced beta1/beta2/weight_decay through base_config.py, train_config.py, train.py, and model.py's configure_optimizers to explain the AdamW hyperparameters actually in use (beta1=0.9, beta2=0.95 default / 0.99 in train_config.py, weight_decay=0.1). Explained beta1 as the first-moment (momentum) decay and beta2 as the second-moment (variance) decay, and walked through why beta2 in LLM pretraining typically sits well below Adam's textbook 0.999 default despite noisy gradients: raising beta2 reduces variance in the v_t estimate (helps with stationary, small-batch noise, which is why train_config.py bumps it to 0.99 for its low tokens-per-iter setting) but also makes v_t lag behind sudden gradient-magnitude spikes, which is destabilizing against the heavy-tailed/bursty gradient noise common in transformer training — the 0.95-0.99 range is a compromise between those two effects. Also explained weight_decay as AdamW's decoupled shrinkage of weights toward zero each step (separate from the gradient-based update, unlike classic L2-in-Adam), and confirmed model.py only applies it to 2D+ parameter tensors (matmul weights, embeddings), excluding biases and norm gains.
- 2026-09-30: No code changes this session. Traced through model.py and train.py to confirm causal masking mechanics: get_batch() (train.py) builds y as x shifted by one token, and CausalSelfAttention applies a triangular mask (is_causal=True on the fast path, explicit masked_fill against the tril `bias` buffer on the fallback path) so every position's attention is restricted to itself and earlier positions. Confirmed the model trains all block_size positions of a sequence in one parallel forward/backward pass (teacher forcing) via a single cross_entropy over logits.view(-1, vocab) vs targets.view(-1), and that ignore_index=-1 in that loss is unused in pretraining since get_batch never emits -1 targets. Also traced batch_size vs gradient_accumulation_steps in train.py: tokens_per_iter = gradient_accumulation_steps * ddp_world_size * batch_size * block_size, confirming batch_size (line 52) is only the memory-bound micro-batch while accumulation simulates the larger effective batch used for the optimizer step (loss divided by gradient_accumulation_steps before backward, line ~304).
